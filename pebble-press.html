About the Project

A charming, minimalist news curation application concept designed to deliver a clutter-free reading experience. Combining a "cute and simple" design aesthetic with clean typography, the prototype reimagines daily information consumption. It features personalized news feeds, digestible summary cards, and intuitive category filtering to keep users updated on current events without media fatigue.
