WAKEY

February 2025 - May 2025

About the Project

An innovative mobile application designed to tackle oversleeping by transforming standard, easily dismissible alarms into interactive mental exercises. Built with a user-centric UI/UX and reliable mobile functionality, Wakey serves as a practical utility for students and professionals striving to meet early morning commitments.

  • Gamified Alarm Mechanics: Replaces traditional snooze buttons with a built-in maze puzzle that users must successfully navigate to silence the audio, ensuring cognitive alertness.
  • Full CRUD Functionality: Allows users to seamlessly create, read, update, and delete multiple highly customizable alarm profiles tailored to their weekly schedules.
